Supplied Gaming weapon references
The sniper rifle cuboids and texture were converted from the user-supplied
"minecraft-sniper-rifle.zip" (Blockbench glTF export; no creator field included).
The Leviathan Axe cuboids and texture were converted from the user-supplied
"GoW_Leviathan128_1_21_1_v2.zip", whose pack description credits Szombie.
Conversions preserve the source cuboid silhouettes and UVs as native item parts.
The reference archives, foreign mod classes, datapack functions and shaders are
not included in this release. Kunai, curved sword, energy sword, hook and
Tung Tung figure geometry are authored locally from the supplied visual references.
The native baseball bat uses the cuboid layout and shape from the user-supplied "Baseballbats.zip" resource pack (Blockbench credit; creator not identified in the archive). The Angry Birds slingshot geometry was converted from the user-supplied Angry Slingshot model; only native item JSON and vanilla wool/oak textures are used. The morning star geometry and texture were converted from the user-supplied "morning-star-updated.zip" Blockbench model (creator not identified in the archive). Source archives and executable mod code are not included in the release.
